## Summary (2026-10-13)

**Setup** (`step0_setup_baselines.md`). Dream-7B family, MATH-500, lockstep decoder @1024 (one token per step, argmax), last 300 problems by default.
"student ⇐ teacher @θ": the student decodes; at steps where its raw token probability is below θ, the teacher's token is written.

**Single models** (last 300 / all 500 / finish rate on last 300)

| model | last 300 | all 500 | finish |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Ins | 36.7 | 39.8 | 76% |
| M(.4,.6) best merge | 42.0 | 46.6 | 81% |
| M(0,1) over merge | 44.0 | 45.6 | 74% |
| A(2.0) over addition | 46.7 | 51.4 | 94% |
| AR Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, strict / lenient | 55.3 / 70.7 | 56.0 / 71.0 | 78% |

**Gated systems** (`step1_methods.md` §1)

| system | last 300 | all 500 | vs teacher (McNemar p) |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| M(.4,.6) ⇐ A(2.0) @0.8 | **51.0** | **54.2** | 0.21 |
| Ins ⇐ A(2.0) @0.6 / @0.8 | 49.3 / 49.0 | 52.2 / 53.0 | n.s. |
| M(.4,.6) ⇐ M(0,1) @0.7† / @0.8 | 48.0 / 47.7 | 50.4 / 50.0 | 0.16 / 0.04 (all 500) |

The gate reaches the teacher and never significantly beats it. A(2.0) as teacher helps through finish rate (81% → 89–95%); M(0,1) as teacher helps
through precision on finished problems (52 → 62) with no finish-rate change. The student's identity barely matters. The weight-space midpoint
(0.7,1.3) does not replace token-level mixing (44.3).

**Mechanism** (`step1_methods.md` §2, all four pre-registered readings met)
- Unconditional random replacement at the gate's own dose = the student (−6 to −11 pts vs the gate, p ≤ 0.03): the confidence signal knows *where* to ask.
- Teacher's decode order alone = the student; order + content ≤ content alone (on M(0,1) it collapses to the teacher). The mechanism is
  "student keeps the order, teacher supplies content at the student's low-confidence steps".
- The earlier "random among disagreements ≈ gate" control was mis-designed (it was handed the disagreement positions for free); relabelled.

**Verdict** (`step2_verdict.md`). Method and mechanism are established; the ceiling is the teacher. The AR reference under lenient scoring is 71,
≈19 points above our best, and the oracle over all 13 systems is 78: no combination inside this family beats AR on MATH. Remaining STEP2 work:
fix every hyperparameter (teacher line, β, θ) by a rule that does not look at the test set, then run the pipeline unchanged on a second model family.
STEP3 = distil the 2× mixture into the 1× student (prompts from MATH train / Tulu-3, targets generated by the mixture, self-RFT control mandatory).
